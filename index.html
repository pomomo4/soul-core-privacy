SOUL CORE: Defense Privacy Policy

App: SOUL CORE: Defense (com.gise.coredefense) · Developer: gise · Effective: 2026-09-27

This game does not collect any personal information. It does not request or read your name, email, phone number, location, device identifiers, contacts, or photos. There is no account or sign-in.

The game does not request the Internet permission and does not communicate with any server. It contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting tools, and sends no information to the developer or any third party.

Game progress (currency, upgrades, stage records, tower layouts) and settings (volume, speed) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. You can also erase it with [New Game] in the game.
